Case Study

Multiplayer Mobile Game

Built on a massively parallel multi-agent platform for task automation (MANTA).

7x faster

24 mo → 3.2 mo

The original project plan required 7 people working for 24 months to build the game. On MANTA: the engine, artwork, client, server, ops and final product shipped in 3.2 months. Adding more people cannot shorten a delivery timeline. Empowering the team with applied intelligence did.

5x cheaper

$2.34M → $443k

248k lines of reviewed, playtested code shipped on MANTA for $443k, labor included. Against the original $2.34M budget, the cost of production dropped from $9.44 per line of tested code to $1.79.

30x output

20,480 h → 680 h

The product was scoped as 700 deliverables, requiring 20,480 hours of work. On MANTA, agents broke them into 48,300 tasks while humans spent 680 hours on higher value judgment and direction, producing 30x more productive output.

A 24-month production timeline was reduced to 3.2 months.

A full production game shipped, not a prototype.
