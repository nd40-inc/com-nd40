Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-24

Short version

Nd40 does not collect personal information through this website. There are no accounts, forms, cookies, analytics or tracking, and no third-party scripts or embeds. Fonts are hosted on the site itself.

What Nd40 collects

Nothing. The site has no way to submit information to us, and we do not set cookies or run analytics.

What our hosting provider processes

The site is hosted on Cloudflare. To deliver and protect the site, Cloudflare processes standard request data, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you request. Cloudflare does this as our hosting provider, under its own terms and policies. See the Cloudflare Privacy Policy for details.

Cookies and tracking

This site does not use cookies or tracking technologies.

Selling and sharing

We do not sell or share personal information, because we do not collect any through the site.

Children

The site is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If we later add forms, analytics or anything else that collects information, we will update this policy before it goes live. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
